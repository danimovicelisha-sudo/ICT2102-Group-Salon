Welcome to Touch Beauty Salon

Touch Beauty Salon is a friendly and professional beauty salon located in Kaliro, Uganda. We provide quality beauty and personal care services for women, men and young people.

Our services include hair styling, hair braiding, wig installation, wig making, manicure, pedicure, makeup and other beauty treatments.

Customers can make an appointment by calling the salon, sending an email or visiting us in person.

Our Story

Touch Beauty Salon is a community-focused beauty salon serving customers in Kaliro, Uganda. Our goal is to provide quality and affordable beauty services in a clean, friendly and welcoming environment.

We believe that beauty is more than appearance. It is also about confidence, self-care and feeling comfortable in your own style.

Our team focuses on customer satisfaction, professionalism, creativity and good personal care.
