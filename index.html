About Touch Beauty Salon

Touch Beauty Salon is a friendly and professional beauty salon serving customers in Kaliro, Uganda. Our goal is to provide quality beauty and personal care services in a clean, comfortable and welcoming environment.

Our salon offers a wide range of services including hair styling, hair braiding, wig installation, wig making, manicure, pedicure, makeup and hair treatments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality and affordable beauty services while helping every customer feel confident, comfortable and valued.

Our Values

  • Professionalism
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Cleanliness
  • Creativity
  • Respect for every customer
  • Quality service

Our Beauty Philosophy

We believe that beauty is an important part of confidence and self-expression. We therefore work to provide services that allow our customers to look good, feel comfortable and express their personal style.
